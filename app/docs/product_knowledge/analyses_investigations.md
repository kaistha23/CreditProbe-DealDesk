## What it is
An analysis is one saved question with its answer, from the Cockpit or What-If, that you can refresh against any period and compare before and after. An investigation is a whole saved thread (Cockpit, What-If, Early Warning or Scorecard) with its artifacts and notes, which you can reopen and continue.

## Flow
1. Under a Cockpit or What-If answer, press **Save as analysis**; under a thread, **Save as investigation**.
2. [Open Analyses](/analyses) and open one; press **Refresh** and pick a period to see the before and after.
3. [Open Investigations](/investigations) to reopen a thread and continue it, or add notes.
4. Share either through Mail or add it to a Playbook report.

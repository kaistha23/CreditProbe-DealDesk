## What it is
The Cockpit is the conversational front door to the corporate and retail books. You ask a question in plain language; the assistant plans the analysis, queries the book, checks every figure against the result and publishes an answer with its tables, charts and the trace of what it ran. The home page also shows what needs attention this period and the ECL panel.

## Flow
1. [Open the Cockpit](/) and choose the book (corporate or retail) with the switch at the top.
2. Read the attention items: each card opens a drawer with the trend, the segment against the book, the contributors and the stage mix.
3. Type a question in the Ask box, for example "Which sectors drove the ECL increase this quarter?". The process panel shows the stages: understanding, planning, querying, validating, publishing.
4. Read the answer: every figure is bound to the query result; open the trace to see the SQL.
5. Follow up in the same thread, or save the answer as an analysis, add it to an investigation, share it through Mail or add it to a Playbook report with the buttons under the answer.

## Good to know
- A thread belongs to one book; switching the book starts a new conversation.
- Ask "How does What-If work?" or "How do I use Projects?" in the Cockpit to get a walkthrough of any module.

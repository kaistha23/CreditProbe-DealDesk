## What it is
Data Domains (formerly Data Builder, in the DATA group of the navigation) builds an extract from the books without SQL: pick the book, the period, the columns and the filters, preview it, and export it to a house-styled Excel file.

## Flow
1. [Open Data Domains](/data-builder) and choose the book and period.
2. Pick the columns and add filters (sector, stage, Early Warning band, exposure range).
3. Preview the rows and the totals.
4. Export to Excel, or add the extract to a Playbook report.

## Good to know
- Extracts read the books read-only; the Excel carries an About sheet with the source and filters.

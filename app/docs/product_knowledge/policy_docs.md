## What it is
Policy Docs holds the credit policy and the circulars. A new policy or circular is read by the assistant, which lists the clauses it found and what they change; once approved, the clauses join the active policy pack that the Cockpit cites.

## Flow
1. [Open Policy Docs](/policy-docs) to read the policy pack and the circulars, clause by clause.
2. Upload a policy or circular (.docx, .pdf or text).
3. Read the interpretation: summary, clauses (ID, limit, threshold, scope), what changes and which books and rules it affects.
4. Answer **Add this to the Cockpit?**: on Yes the clauses join the active pack with an effective date and the approver.
5. Policy questions in the Cockpit now cite the new clauses.

## Good to know
- The active **Regulatory** document is the **CBUAE Credit Risk Management Standards** (Central Bank of the UAE, UAE), in the active pack from **pp-2026.4** (effective 12 Oct 2026). Its paragraphs keep the Standards' numbering, by Article (**CBUAE CRM Art. 7.8 DPD indicator**, **CBUAE CRM Art. 9.23**): credit risk governance, the framework, oversight functions, underwriting, the definition of default, SICR, restructuring, classification and provisioning, credit risk mitigation and collateral, portfolio management and reporting, non-performing assets and write-off, and models. [Open the Standards](/policy-docs?doc=pd_cbuae_crm)
- The Cockpit quotes them alongside the Bank's clause a question reaches (for example **RP-4.2** with **CBUAE CRM Art. 8.3–9.35** for restructured loans, **RP-5.1** with **CBUAE CRM Art. 7.8** for SICR), with the pack version. The Standards set no single-obligor or sector limit (they require Board approval above 10% of Tier 1 capital, **CBUAE CRM Art. 2.6**); the limit in force is the Bank's **CP-1.1**.
- **Breach checks**: CreditProbe checks the books against the Standards (90+ days past due not in Stage 3, 30+ days in Stage 1, wholesale cross-default, Stage 3 exits and probation, restructured exposures in Stage 1, the Stage 3 provision floors, collateral haircuts and valuations, Stage 3 over 5 years, the Stage 3 ratio). Every breach names its article, the facility or account, the value measured and required, and its EAD and ECL. Ask any assistant "Which exposures breach the CBUAE CRM standards?", or open the **CBUAE CRM compliance** card on the [Early Warning home](/early-warning?domain=corporate).
- CBO circular **BM-1230** (Oman) is not in force for the Bank: it stays in the library as **Withdrawn**, with its history (pack pp-2026.3). [Open BM-1230](/policy-docs?doc=pd_cbo_bm1230)

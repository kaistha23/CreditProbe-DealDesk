## What it is
Deal Desk (pre-deal analysis) prices and structures a corporate facility before approval: risk (PD, LGD, EAD, ECL), capital (SAMA standardised RWA), revenue and cost, lifetime RAROC and SVA, the marginal portfolio limits against the live book, and an optimiser that proposes counter-offers that clear the hurdle within the limits.

## Flow
1. Open Deal Desk and start a proposal in the chat, from the form, or by uploading a credit memo; an existing borrower is pre-filled from the books.
2. Read the analysis: RAROC, SVA, RWA, EL, the breakeven margin and the limit headroom.
3. Press **Optimise** (in the chat bar at the bottom) for the recommended term sheet, ranked counter-offers, 2 to 4 alternative structures over the full lever set with *why it works*, and the minimum change on each single lever (see Deal Desk levers).
4. Ask why, change a lever, save the analysis, export the Word report (proposal in full first, method appendix last) or add it to a Playbook report.
5. Asking on the home opens the Deal Desk thread with the process trace; **Recent analyses** lists every saved or optimised analysis with a link back to its thread.

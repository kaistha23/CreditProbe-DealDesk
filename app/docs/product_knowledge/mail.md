## What it is
Mail is CreditProbe's internal mail: share any object (a Cockpit answer, a report, a scenario, a borrower page) with colleagues, with the object attached as a live card.

## Flow
1. Press **Share** on any object, pick the recipients and add a note.
2. [Open Mail](/messages) to read the inbox, reply with quotes, star, label and search.
3. Open the attached card to land on the object itself.

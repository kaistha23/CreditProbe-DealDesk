## What it is
Settings holds the bank profile (name, reporting currency, fiscal calendar, as-of periods), the house style every Excel and Word export uses, the register of owned parameter tables (Deal Desk, Early Warning rule set, What-If models, Scorecard thresholds) with owner and version, notification and mail settings, the status of each data feed, and retention.

## Flow
1. [Open Settings](/settings).
2. Change a setting: it is submitted for approval.
3. In Parameter tables, press Propose change on a table: a draft follows the Parameter-table change workflow (owner, Model Validation, CRO).
4. Check Data feeds for each book's release and load time.

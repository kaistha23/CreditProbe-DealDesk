## What it is
Playbook is where reports are written: credit committee packs, risk committee reports, portfolio reviews, borrower credit memos, scenario reports and validation reports. An assistant discusses the report with you, drafts every section from the books, and each section has its own assistant reading the data that section needs.

## Flow
1. [Open Playbook](/playbook) and tell the chat at the top what you need, for example "I want to draft a corporate ECL report". Asking opens a thread: the conversation in the middle, the chat box pinned at the bottom with **Create report** beside it.
2. The assistant proposes the scope and the sections, each with what goes in and where its data comes from. Change it ("add a section on concentrations", "drop the scenarios") or ask about the data.
3. Press **Create report** (or say "Create it"): the report is created and every section is drafted from the books.
4. Scroll to a section and open its **Section assistant**: brainstorm what must go in, ask questions (the Early Warning section reads Early Warning, the scenario section What-If, and so on), import a worked What-If scenario with its waterfall, and press **Add to section** (beside the section's chat box) to add the latest answer.
5. Use the right-hand assistant to discuss the whole report ("what is still missing?") or to move it on: send for review, set reviewers and a due date.
6. Track the status (Draft, In review, Approved, Presented), comments and versions, export the house-styled Word document and share it through Mail.

## Good to know
- Say **Add the CBUAE CRM compliance summary** to import the breaches of the CBUAE Credit Risk Management Standards by article, with the largest breaches, into a report.
- "Add to report" anywhere in CreditProbe lands here with its charts and tables.
- Every assistant answer, in the top thread, the right-hand assistant and each section assistant, shows the process trace with its running timer while it works.

## What it is
The Audit log records who did what and when across CreditProbe: sign-ins, views as another user, exports, approvals and rejections, configuration changes, kill switches and every AI run. It merges the Admin service, the Core service (sign-in and Mail) and the assistants' run log.

## Flow
1. [Open the Audit log](/audit-log).
2. Filter by person, kind of event or words.
3. Export to Excel (house style); the export itself is audited.

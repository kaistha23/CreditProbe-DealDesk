## What it is
My workspace is each user's home page: what is waiting on you (tasks, mentions, unread mail, reports to review, critical Early Warning names on your books) and your analyses, investigations and projects. Every row opens the page that owns it.

## Flow
1. Sign in: you land on [My workspace](/workspace).
2. Work down the panels; each row opens its page.

## What it is
Early Warning scores every corporate facility each quarter and every retail account each month against a published rule set, and bands each name none, low, moderate, high or critical with the reasons that fired. It finds deterioration before it shows in staging and lists the names to review.

## Flow
1. [Open Early Warning](/early-warning?domain=corporate) and pick the book.
2. Read the bands: names and exposure by band against the prior period, and the top reasons.
3. Open a sector (corporate) or product (retail) card to see its trend and its high and critical names.
4. Open a name: the customer page shows the score history, the reasons, the facilities and the collateral; it links to [Borrower 360](/borrower-360).
5. Export the list to Excel, save it to an investigation, or send the segment to What-If to stress it.

## Good to know
- The portfolio home carries a **CBUAE CRM compliance** card: the breaches of the CBUAE Credit Risk Management Standards on the book at its latest period, by article (for example **CBUAE CRM Art. 9.23** Stage 3 provision below the wholesale floor), with EAD, ECL, the provision shortfall and the largest breaches.
- A client's band is its worst facility's band; the rule set and its version are in the model card.
- High and critical bands follow staging and default triggers, so high-alert corporate names sit in Stage 2 or 3.

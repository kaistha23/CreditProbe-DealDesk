## What it is
Users and Teams holds everyone who signs in to CreditProbe: their role (Admin, CRO, Credit Risk Analyst, RM, Auditor, Model Validator), team, RM ID, the access policies that apply to them and their last activity, plus the RM and branch hierarchy from the Branches and RMs domain.

## Flow
1. [Open Users and Teams](/users).
2. Change a user's role or team, invite a user or deactivate one: each is submitted for approval.
3. Reset a password: the new one is written to the local users file and the user's sessions end.
4. See Teams, Roles and the RM hierarchy in their tabs.

## Good to know
- A deactivated user keeps their history but opens no module.

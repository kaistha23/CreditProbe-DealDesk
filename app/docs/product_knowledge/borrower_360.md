## What it is
Borrower 360 is the single view of one corporate borrower or retail customer: profile, rating and outlook, facilities, exposure, ECL and stage, collateral, covenants, Early Warning, and the relationship graph (group, guarantors, suppliers and buyers).

## Flow
1. [Open Borrower 360](/borrower-360) and search by ID or name, or arrive from an Early Warning list or a Cockpit answer.
2. Read the profile tab: rating, sector, region, exposure, ECL, stage and the Early Warning band with its reasons.
3. Open the facilities and collateral tabs for the detail by facility.
4. Open the relationships tab to see the group and connected parties; export it to Excel.
5. Add the page to a Playbook credit memo with "Add to report".

## Good to know
- Every figure is the book's at the reporting date; the IDs are the books' IDs (CB…, RC…).

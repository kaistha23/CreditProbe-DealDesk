# Anatomy of a GCC bank credit proposal (learned from two anonymised proposals, 19 Oct 2026)

Anne shared photographs of two real credit proposals from a UAE bank (a renewal for a building-materials trading group, and a new project-specific limit for a construction contractor). **No name, figure or reference from them is committed.** This file records only their structure, analytical coverage and voice, so CreditProbe's drafter (R12, I25) and committee sheet (I27) can reproduce them. Fictional exemplars rebuilt on this skeleton go in `data/dealdesk/house_style/exemplars/` (R12 build).

## 1. Two voices in one document

| Part | Author | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| **Action requested** (page 1) | Business (corporate banking group) | What is asked: facilities, terms, security, conditions |
| **Credit division comments** (pages 2–n) | Credit division, independent | Facts, adjusted figures, doubts, exceptions |
| **Credit division recommendation** (last page) | Credit division | Key strengths, numbered recommendation, added conditions |
| **CRO review / comments**, **Credit committee approval / instructions** | CRO, committee (approval authority named: CC level, Board) | Blank boxes signed by hand |

Business proposes, credit disposes. CreditProbe's "Prepare" (RM) and "Review" (credit) views (I17) map exactly to these two voices.

## 2. Section skeleton (in order)

1. **Header block on every page**: branch/unit, customer, date account opened, CIF/base no., group no., related or connected party flag with party CIF, date, AO, RO, purpose code, new / existing / renewal / additional / amendment ticks, review date, industry code, collateral code, existing and proposed rating.
2. **Action requested**: one paragraph naming every ask (renew, reduce, new one-off limit, increase group exposure cap, with the previous committee reference).
3. **Facilities table**: per limit and sub-limit (general limit; project limit 1..n; each with APG, PB, RB, LC sight/usance, LCM sub-limit, DMCCM against payment certificates, DMCCM supplier payments, VHM, one-off LGs against cash). Columns: existing, outstanding, proposed, cash margin, particulars (commission, validity, profit rate as benchmark + spread with floor, tenor and repayment). Totals. Study and documentation fee.
4. **Security and support**, per limit group: personal and corporate guarantees (with net-worth statement date), undated security cheques, assignment of receivables / insurance / project proceeds (with perfection deadline), hypothecation, subordination of shareholder accounts and loans, NOCs, undertakings.
5. **Terms and conditions**, numbered, per limit group: utilisation rules (who may use, against what documents), caps (single LG cap, revolving utilisation cap, max outstanding at any time), purpose restrictions (no inter-group, no related parties), disbursement rules (against certified payment certificates, % financed, last certificates not financed), settlement waterfall (proceeds first settle related liabilities), cover ratio (funded outstanding covered ≥ x% by remaining contract proceeds), reporting covenants (audited within n days, interim within n days), business routing undertaking (x times the limit), auditor change, and **status of existing conditions ("not complied")**.
6. **Group exposure** (funded / total) and **references** (credit file request dates, revisions, emails).
7. **Background**: trade licence (incorporation date and place, activity, legal status); shareholders table (name, age/DOB, nationality, %, beneficial owner); parent entity and its shareholders; recent ownership changes; group entities and what each does; activity mix, sales terms (PDC / LC / open account %), purchase terms, staff count.
8. **Relationship with the bank**: since when, account turnover and average balance, history of limits with committee references, utilisation per limit against cap, past dues in the last 12 months, returned cheques.
9. **Status of financed projects** (contractors): employer, consultant, contract value original and revised, start and completion, work done %, amounts received and due, last payment certificate; one paragraph per project on delays, extensions of time (formalised or not), payment delays, demand letters.
10. **Central bank risk bureau (CBRBR)** table for the subject and every related entity: funded limit / outstanding, non-funded limit / outstanding, total, number of banks, classification.
11. **Credit bureau (AECB / SIMAH)** per entity and UBO: outstanding, overdue, returned cheques with dates, score, court cases; which reports were not provided and why.
12. **Other banks' facilities**: matrix of bank × group entity; reconciliation of client-declared vs bureau figures and what was not clarified.
13. **New project details** (project finance): scope, employer, main contractor, consultant, contract value, LG requirements, payment terms, tenor, expected margin; **project cash flow** (cost breakdown by category, retention, advance, cumulative deficit and peak month, stressed for payment delays); **about the project employer** (ownership, listing, size, bureau).
14. **Financials**: auditor (listed / unlisted panel), opinion (qualified, with the **basis of qualification** summarised), which entities are combined; 3-year table plus interim for subject, combined group and consolidated parent: revenue / NP / NPM, GP / GPM, OP / OPM, total assets / ROA, bank debt ST / LT, net worth / ROE / leverage, EBITDA / gearing, working capital / current ratio, cash cycle / operating cash flow.
15. **Financial synopsis** (bullets): growth with prior year in brackets; composition of current assets with %; receivables ageing and concentration (top n %); retention receivables; due from related parties; liabilities; **adjusted** net worth, leverage, working capital and current ratio (netting intangibles, related-party balances, shareholder current accounts, non-core investments); contingent liabilities vs project value; operating cash flow; interim results annualised; projections; parent's consolidated view, including deleveraging and asset sales.
16. **Effects of the current geopolitical / market situation** on the business (routes, ports, input costs, labour).
17. **Exceptions** (policy deviations, each with the reason business gives) and **other observations**: missing documents, unperfected guarantees, legal-opinion age, auditor quality, covenant breaches, insider or staff shareholding in a guarantor, related parties classified doubtful or in litigation, concentration of backlog and receivables, execution capacity (backlog vs revenue), LG wording risks (operative clause, beneficiary format, open-ended), what business says vs what credit could verify.
18. **Key strengths** (3–4 bullets).
19. **Recommendation**: numbered decisions, group exposure, security, **additional conditions** (conditions precedent "prior to limit loading", undertakings, monitoring duties for business and regional head).
20. **Signature blocks**: analyst / senior manager, VP credit, CCO; CRO comments; committee instructions with approval authority.

## 3. Voice (what makes each sentence valuable)

- **Every sentence carries a fact, a number and a date.** Prior-year comparatives in brackets: "rose to X (PY Y)".
- **Shares, not just amounts**: "68% of current assets", "top 2 = 83%".
- **Attribution and evidence status are explicit**: "as per business", "business conveys", "reportedly", "not shared", "not provided", "not clarified", "not explained", "copies not provided", "attributed to reporting error".
- **Independent re-performance**: "if X is netted off, adjusted net worth is Y and leverage Z".
- **Measured scepticism**: "We note that…", "Upon checking…", "yet the same is reported as not due", "which clearly indicates…". Key concerns are underlined.
- **History and references**: committee decision numbers and dates, previous caps, earlier conditions and whether they were met.
- **Terse, formal, dense**; no adjectives without a number behind them; no marketing language.
- **Conditions are operational**: who does what, by when, measured how ("within 2 months of LG issuance", "prior to limit loading", "dually by RM and regional head").

SAUDI CENTRAL BANK (SAMA) - CIRCULAR No. 47/2026 (SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT)
Prudential requirements for financing to construction and contracting companies
This circular is a synthetic document written for testing CreditProbe. It is not issued by SAMA.
This circular is effective from 1 December 2026.

1. Scope
1.1 This circular applies to all corporate financing that banks extend to construction and contracting companies, including term loans, revolving facilities and guarantee lines.

2. Debt service capacity
2.1 Banks shall not extend new financing to a construction or contracting company whose debt service coverage ratio (DSCR), after the new financing, is below 1.40x.
2.2 For construction and contracting companies, total debt to EBITDA after the new financing must not exceed 3.50x.

3. Collateral
3.1 Valuations of collateral pledged by construction and contracting companies must be dated within 6 months of the credit decision.

4. Reporting
4.1 Banks shall report their exposure to the construction sector to SAMA every quarter, by obligor group.

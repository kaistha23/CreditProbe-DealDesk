GOLD-STANDARD CREDIT PROPOSAL (SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE)
1. ACTION REQUESTED
The obligor requests renewal of its facilities of SAR 120.0 mn and a new term facility of SAR 40.0 mn for 5 years. The obligor is rated BB+ internally. Approval is sought from the Credit Committee.
2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The obligor is a ready-mix and building materials trader in the Eastern Province. Revenue was SAR 410.0 mn in 2025. EBITDA was SAR 52.0 mn. The obligor routes 70% of its turnover through the Bank. The facility funds a new batching plant.
3. FACILITIES
Total limits move from SAR 120.0 mn to SAR 160.0 mn. The new term facility amortises over 5 years after a 12-month grace period.
4. SECURITY AND SUPPORT
The facilities are secured by a mortgage over the Dammam yard valued at SAR 95.0 mn. The obligor's owner gives a personal guarantee.
5. KEY RISKS AND MITIGANTS
Risk | Mitigant
Concentration on two contractors | Assignment of contract proceeds; routing covenant of 70%
Rising working capital | Borrowing base capped at 75% of eligible receivables
6. FINANCIAL ANALYSIS
DSCR after the new facility is 1.6x. Debt to EBITDA is 2.9x. The obligor's current ratio is 1.3x.
7. CONDUCT OF ACCOUNT
SIMAH shows no defaults for the obligor and its guarantor. The obligor's accounts show no returned cheques in 24 months.
8. KEY STRENGTHS
Twenty years with the Bank; diversified buyers; audited accounts with an unqualified opinion.
9. RECOMMENDATION
The Credit division recommends approval of the renewal and the new facility, subject to the conditions above.
